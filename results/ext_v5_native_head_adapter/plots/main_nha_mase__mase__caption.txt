In-context MASE of Chronos-2's frozen native Quantile Head when the encoder is truncated at each representation point. “Linear adapter” inserts a single $768{\times}768$ linear map $A_\ell$ before the frozen final RMSNorm and the frozen native head; each $A_\ell$ is trained separately on that dataset, and the rest of the model is never updated. “Fresh linear probe” is an independently fitted $Q{=}1$ readout on the same states. Bands are 95% paired series-cluster bootstrap CIs.
