1. CONVENTIONS (as implemented, not re-chosen)
----------------------------------------------
- Depth axis: L0 (state before block 1) .. final block (L12 / L20 / L12). Chronos-2 L12+LN and
  TiRex L12+RMS are head-input diagnostics (point_type head_input_diagnostic), excluded from the
  depth axis, the tunnel and the main CKA matrices (cka_*.npy vs cka_*__with_head_input.npy).
- CKA: unbiased linear CKA ("headline_estimator": "unbiased"), TEST split (the committed
  Chronos-2 CKA convention), raw hidden states (phase1.geometry_block refuses z-scored input).
- Effective rank: exp(entropy of normalized squared singular values of the centred matrix),
  TRAIN split (the committed spectral convention; the run stores both, test is a robustness curve).
- Representations: Chronos-2 K=4 forecast-slot states stacked (n*4 rows, d=768); TimesFM-3 last
  real context token (n rows, d=1280); TiRex headline "pos0" = pass-0 readout token (n rows,
  d=512); TiRex "all_positions_from_L1" (2n rows, L1..L12) is a diagnostic only.
- Chronos-2 L0 forecast-slot state is near-degenerate (effective rank ~1.0 on every dataset), so
  CKA involving L0 is uninformative for Chronos-2.
- The biased CKA estimator is not used here (its floor differs across models; phase1 caveat).


2. POST-HOC DESCRIPTORS (exploratory; not part of the registered Phase-1 analysis)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No "geometric transition" rule exists in the Phase-1 code, so I defined a few parameter-free
landmarks, each with a chance baseline, instead of a thresholded transition detector:
  (a) erank peak: argmax of the train effective-rank curve.
  (b) dominant CKA boundary: the single split of L1..L into two contiguous blocks maximizing
      mean within-block CKA minus mean between-block CKA (L0 excluded: degenerate for
      Chronos-2). Also computed over L1..L-1 ("excl. final") so TiRex's always-distinct final
      block cannot dominate.
  (c) entry step: the adjacent step (l*-1 -> l*); its rank among all L steps by 1-CKA and by
      |delta erank|.
  (d) sharpest CKA step: the adjacent step k-1 -> k (k = 2..L; L0 -> L1 excluded) with the lowest
      CKA; "inside the tunnel" if k > l*.
  (e) share of erank total variation sum|r_j - r_(j-1)| from steps entering depths > l*;
      erank final/peak; CKA(l*, L).
  "near" = within max(1, round(0.1 L)) blocks: 1 block for L=12, 2 for L=20.
  Chance baselines assume the landmark is placed uniformly over its admissible depths,
  independently of l*.
Robustness across splits: erank peak train==test in 25/38 (Chronos-2 12/13, TimesFM-3 7/12,
  TiRex 6/13 -- TiRex peaks are flat plateaus); dominant CKA boundary train vs test within 1 block
  in 35/38 (TimesFM-3 9/12). TimesFM-3's CKA block structure is weak and split-dependent: treat
  its boundary locations as unreliable.


3. EVIDENCE SUMMARY
-------------------
Short version: the two-way dissociation IS supported, as a statement about depth location, with
one important nuance (dataset-level covariation in Chronos-2) and one model-specific pattern
(TiRex's final block).

3.1 The entrance is not placed at a geometric landmark (31 pairs with l* > L0).
  - Entry step is the largest adjacent change: CKA 3/31, erank 2/31; chance 2.3.
  - Erank peak within +-0.1L of l*: 2/31 (chance 6.9). Peak before / near / after the entrance:
    12 / 2 / 17 (Chronos-2 5/0/7, TimesFM-3 0/1/7, TiRex 7/1/3).
  - Dominant CKA boundary within +-0.1L of l*: 8/31 (chance 6.8); excluding the final block
    5/31 (chance 6.9). The 5: Chronos-2 London (L3 vs L4), TiRex London (L8 vs L8), TimesFM-3
    Uber (L1 vs L2), Coastal (L5 vs L5), Electricity 15min (L1 vs L2; weak boundaries).
  => Co-location of the entrance with any geometric landmark is at or below chance.

3.2 Substantial geometric change continues inside the tunnel (38 pairs).
  - The sharpest adjacent CKA change among block outputs lies strictly inside the tunnel in 33/38
    (Chronos-2 10/13, TimesFM-3 12/12, TiRex 11/13); uniform-placement expectation 23.3. Among
    the 17 late entrances (l*/L >= 0.5), still 12/17.
  - TiRex: the sharpest CKA step is L11 -> L12 in 13/13 datasets (adjacent CKA 0.31-0.92 over all
    13; 0.31-0.76 in the 11 with l* <= L11, e.g. London 0.31, Electricity 0.41, SG Carpark 0.51), with erank collapsing across it (London
    66 -> 9, BOOM 64 -> 2, Uber TLC 41 -> 3). In the 11 datasets whose entrance is <= L11,
    val(L11)/val(L12) lies in [0.989, 1.023]: the largest representational change on the depth
    axis moves validation loss by at most 2.3%.
  - Effective rank rises and then collapses toward the final block in every model; median
    final/peak = 0.11 (Chronos-2 0.07, TimesFM-3 0.20, TiRex 0.09). The peak is at or after the
    entrance in 26/38 pairs (chance 23.3 -- i.e. not aligned with the entrance, but it means the
    entire decline lies inside the tunnel in those 26).
  - >= 50% of the erank total variation occurs inside the tunnel in 27/38 (20/31 excluding L0
    entrances). CKA(l*, L) < 0.5 in 19/38 (13/31).
  Caveat: "inside the tunnel" is favoured by early entrances; this is why each count is given
  with its chance baseline. The claim that survives is existential and prevalent -- large,
  late geometric reorganization repeatedly occurs within the 5% band -- not that it is aligned
  with the tunnel.

3.3 Nuance: geometry and entrance co-vary ACROSS DATASETS in Chronos-2, without aligning in depth.
  Late-entrance datasets (l* = L8-L11: M4, Electricity, SG Carpark, Electricity 15min, Traffic)
  all have the erank peak at L6 and the dominant CKA boundary at L3-L4, i.e. 2-5 (peak) and
  5-7 (boundary) blocks BEFORE the entrance. Early-entrance datasets (l* = L0-L3: Wind Farms, Coastal T-S, BOOM, SZ Taxi, M5,
  Wiki Daily) peak at L8 with the boundary at L10-L12, i.e. AFTER the entrance. Uber TLC and
  London are mixed. So the Chronos-2 geometric profile is dataset-dependent and not independent
  of the functional profile, but in neither group does the entrance coincide with the geometric
  landmarks. This must not be written as "geometry is unrelated to recoverability".

3.4 Other exceptions / cautions.
  - TiRex M4 and Traffic have l* = L12 (final block). There the entrance IS the TiRex final-block
    transition (adjacent CKA 0.92 / 0.69; loss ratio at L11 1.068 / 1.071). These two are the
    cases where recoverability and the dominant geometric change coincide.
  - Chronos-2 Electricity: the entry step (L8 -> L9) is the largest erank step (45.7 peak at L6,
    decline through L9) -- one of the 2/31 erank coincidences.
  - L0 entrances (7 pairs) trivially place all geometric change inside the tunnel; statistics are
    reported with and without them.
  - Absolute CKA / erank are not compared across models (different n, d, readouts).
  - Loss curves: validation is used for the entrance; test curves agree closely for the chosen
    examples, except Chronos-2 SG Carpark (test already within 4% from L3; the validation entrance
    is L10) -- mention if SG Carpark is used.

6. DRAFT TEXT
-------------

\subsection{Geometry and forecast recoverability}
\label{sec:results_geometry}

We next ask whether the onset of the forecasting tunnel coincides with a transition in
representation geometry. For every model--dataset pair with a completed tunnel (38 of 42), we
compare the validation-selected 5\% entrance with the depth profiles of unbiased linear CKA and
entropy effective rank, computed from the same raw readout states the probes use.
Figure~\ref{fig:geometry_main} shows two datasets; all pairs are in
Appendix~\ref{app:geometry}.

\paragraph{Recoverability can begin without a geometric transition.}
On SG Carpark, the Chronos-2 tunnel begins at L10. The dominant change in CKA structure occurs
at L3--L4 and effective rank peaks at L6, whereas representations from L5 onward already have
CKA $\geq 0.87$ with the final block, and adjacent blocks around L10 have CKA $\geq 0.99$. The
TimesFM-3 tunnel on the same dataset begins at L12, where similarity to the final block is near
its minimum (0.51--0.53) and changes little between adjacent blocks. For TiRex, similarity to the
final block is flat ($\approx 0.50$) from L3 to L11 and the tunnel begins at L11. The entrance
is marked by an effective-rank step, however, so no dataset shows this pattern cleanly for all
three models. The pattern is nevertheless general in the aggregate. Among the 31 pairs whose
entrance lies after L0, the step into the entrance is the largest adjacent change in CKA in 3
pairs and in effective rank in 2, as often as expected if entrances were placed at random along
the depth axis (2.3). The effective-rank peak lies within $\pm 0.1L$ of the entrance in 2 pairs
(6.9 expected), and the dominant CKA block boundary in 8 (6.8 expected).

\paragraph{Geometry keeps changing inside the tunnel.}
Conversely, large geometric changes regularly occur after validation loss has already come
within 5\% of the final block. On London Smart Meters, the tunnel begins at L3 (Chronos-2), L1
(TimesFM-3) and L8 (TiRex). In all three models, effective rank subsequently peaks and then
collapses (Chronos-2 39\,$\to$\,90\,$\to$\,8; TimesFM-3 43\,$\to$\,120\,$\to$\,56; TiRex
63\,$\to$\,66\,$\to$\,9), and the entrance representation shares only 0.27--0.33 CKA with the
final block. Across all 38 pairs, the sharpest adjacent change in CKA among block outputs lies
inside the tunnel in 33 pairs, against 23.3 expected at random. For TiRex, it is the final block
in every dataset. In the 11 datasets where the tunnel has already begun, this block has
adjacent CKA 0.31--0.76 yet changes validation loss by at most 2.3\%. Effective rank falls to a median of
11\% of its peak by the final block, and in 26 pairs the whole decline lies inside the tunnel.

These are statements about depth location, not about independence. In Chronos-2, datasets with
late entrances (L8--L11) share an earlier geometric profile (effective-rank peak at L6, CKA
boundary at L3--L4) than datasets with early entrances (peak at L8). But in neither group does
the entrance coincide with these landmarks. The two coincide only for TiRex on M4 and Traffic,
where the tunnel begins at the final block itself.
\textit{The forecasting tunnel does not correspond to a fixed transition in representation
geometry: near-final forecasting performance can become recoverable without a corresponding
geometric transition, while substantial geometric changes can occur without further gains in
forecast recoverability.}
